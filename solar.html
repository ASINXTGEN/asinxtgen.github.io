Solar Vehicle Prototype

A solar-assisted vehicle built and field-tested on a production vehicle between July and October 2021: a flexible photovoltaic array on the vehicle roof and hood, an MPPT charge controller, and an inline power analyzer logging real output on the road, in the rain, and at night.

Array

Flexible monocrystalline panels mounted first across the vehicle roof, then refined into a two-panel hood array with polarity-marked series and parallel wiring.

Power electronics

MPPT solar charge controller (16–50 V input) feeding the onboard system, with a high-precision watt meter logging volts, amps, watts and watt-hours.

Field testing

Road tests, heavy-rain exposure, and night-time readouts to check output, sealing and wiring under real conditions.
